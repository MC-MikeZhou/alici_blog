# News 类内容规范

## 规范目标

创作高质量的 News/Announcement 文章，实现：
- 清晰传达新功能、更新和公司新闻
- 建立兴奋感，推动新功能采用
- 在品牌+功能关键词上获得排名
- 将读者转化为活跃用户或升级客户

---

## 一、文章结构设计

### 必须包含的章节（按顺序）

#### **1. Hook/Announcement（开篇公告，150 词）**

**作用：** 快速传达新闻，建立兴奋感

**必须包含：**
- 直接说明"什么是新的"（lead with the news）
- 一句话价值主张
- 核心优势的快速预告
- 创造兴奋感/紧迫感

**示例开篇：**
```
Big news: AI Batch Processing is now live on alici.ai. Process up to 100 photos
at once and save hours of manual work. Whether you're creating headshots for
your entire team or experimenting with multiple styles, this game-changing
feature transforms how you use AI portraits.
```

**语气特点：**
- ✓ 直接、积极、充满能量
- ✓ 使用"now live", "just launched", "available today"
- ✗ 不要埋葬 lead（不要拖到第二段才说重点）

---

#### **2. The Problem We're Solving（问题背景，200 词）**

**作用：** 建立背景，让读者产生共鸣

**必须包含：**
- 描述此功能解决的用户痛点
- 说明当前的局限性或变通方法
- 建立"为什么这很重要"的上下文

**示例结构：**
```
Until now, processing multiple photos meant uploading and downloading one at
a time—a tedious process that could take hours for large projects. Teams
creating company headshots had to process each employee individually. Content
creators experimenting with different AI styles wasted time on repetitive tasks.

We've heard this feedback loud and clear: you need a faster way to work at scale.
```

---

#### **3. Introducing [Feature Name]（功能介绍，300 词）**

**作用：** 详细说明新功能是什么

**必须包含：**
- 功能的详细说明
- 3 个核心特性/能力
- 与现有解决方案的区别
- 谁最受益

**结构示例：**
```markdown
## Introducing AI Batch Processing

AI Batch Processing lets you upload up to 100 photos at once and apply the
same AI transformation to all of them simultaneously. It's perfect for
businesses, photographers, and content creators who need consistent results
across multiple images.

**Key Features:**

• **Bulk Upload**: Select up to 100 photos in a single upload
• **Consistent Styling**: Apply the same AI portrait style to all images
• **Smart Processing**: Advanced algorithms ensure quality across the batch
• **Quick Export**: Download all results in one click

**Who Benefits:**
- HR teams creating employee headshots
- Photographers offering AI enhancement services
- Content creators building portrait galleries
- Anyone processing multiple photos regularly
```

---

#### **4. How It Works（使用方法，300-400 词）**

**作用：** 展示实际操作流程

**必须包含：**
- 3-5 步使用流程
- 界面/视觉描述
- 预期结果

**步骤格式：**
```markdown
## How It Works

**Step 1: Select Batch Mode**
Navigate to the AI Portrait creator and click "Batch Processing" in the top menu.

**Step 2: Upload Your Photos**
Drag and drop up to 100 photos or click to select from your device. The system
accepts JPG, PNG, and HEIC formats.

**Step 3: Choose Your Style**
Select from our library of AI portrait styles. This style will be applied
consistently across all uploaded photos.

**Step 4: Process and Review**
Click "Start Processing" and watch the progress bar. Processing 100 photos
typically takes 3-5 minutes.

**Step 5: Download Results**
Once complete, preview results in the gallery view and download all images in
a single ZIP file.
```

**配图需求：** 功能界面截图（标注关键按钮/区域）

#### **5. Real-World Use Cases（实际应用，300 词）**

**作用：** 让读者看到具体价值

**必须包含：**
- 2-3 个具体场景
- 每个场景：用户类型 + 使用方式 + 达成结果

**示例结构：**
```markdown
## Real-World Use Cases

**For HR Teams: Company Headshot Day**
Sarah, an HR manager at a 50-person startup, used Batch Processing to create

taken during onboarding and processed them all with the "Corporate Professional"

under 10 minutes.

**For Photographers: Client Galleries**
Mike, a photographer, offers AI enhancement as an add-on service. With Batch

**For Content Creators: Style Experiments**

```

## 二、写作风格指南

### 语气和用词

**语气特点：**
- **兴奋但可信**：展示热情，但不夸大
- **优势导向**：强调用户获得的好处，而非仅描述功能
- **透明诚实**：承认局限性，建立信任

**用词原则：**
- ✓ 使用力量词："transform", "revolutionize", "streamline"
- ✓ 具体说明优势，不只是功能："Save 5 hours/week" vs. "Faster processing"
- ✓ 现在时态表示可用性："is now live", "ships today"
- ✓ 主动语态："transforms your workflow" not "your workflow is transformed"
- ✓ 直接称呼："you can now..." not "users can now..."
- ✗ 空洞的超级词："revolutionary", "game-changing"（除非真的是）

**示例对比：**

❌ **过度承诺：**
"Our revolutionary AI Batch Processing will completely transform your entire
workflow and solve all your problems. It's the most amazing feature ever created!"

✅ **兴奋但可信：**
"AI Batch Processing saves hours on repetitive tasks. Upload 100 photos at once,
apply consistent AI styling, and download results in minutes. Perfect for teams
and professionals working at scale."

---

### CTA 密度

**News 类文章需要更高的 CTA 频率：**

**理想分布：** 每 300-400 词一次 CTA

**CTA 位置：**
1. "Introducing" 章节后
2. "How It Works" 章节后
3. "Use Cases" 章节后
4. 最终结论

**CTA 变化：**
- 避免重复使用相同文案
- 变化动词："Try", "Start", "Explore", "Discover"
- 结合上下文调整

## 三、内容深度要求

### 字数要求

- **英文最终稿**：1,200-1,800 词
- **中文初稿**：1,400-2,000 字
- **各部分占比**：
  - Hook/公告：~10%
  - 问题背景：~15%
  - 功能介绍：~20%
  - 使用方法：~25%
  - 应用场景：~20%
  - 开始使用/未来展望：~10%

---

### 信息准确性

**必须验证：**
- **功能描述**：与产品团队确认准确性
- **定价/可用性**：核实当前信息
- **访问路径**：实际测试能否访问
- **优势声明**：确保可实现（不夸大）

**特别注意：**
- 功能名称保持完全一致
- 定价/可用性信息精确
- 不含糊说明访问要求或限制

---

### 视觉元素需求

**图片数量：** 2-4 张

**必须包含：**
- 功能 UI 截图（主要）
- Before/After 对比（如适用）
- 使用场景插图（可选）

**Alt 文本要求：**
- 包含功能名称
- 描述具体界面元素
- 示例："Batch Processing interface showing upload panel with 100 photo slots"

**注：** 具体图片规范见《配图规范》（待后续补充）

## 四、SEO 优化要点

### 关键词策略

**关键词密度：**
- 目标：**1.5-2%**（略高于 Tutorial/List，用于品牌认知）
- 品牌关键词（alici.ai）分布：
  - Introduction
  - 正文 2-3 次
  - Conclusion
- 功能名称分布：
  - Title 和 H6 标题
  - 正文 3-5 次
  - 自然融入，不强塞

---

### H6 标题策略

**数量：** 5-6 个 H6 标题

**优化原则：**
- 2-3 个标题包含功能名称
- 使用行动语言："Introducing", "How It Works", "Getting Started"
- 传达进展和价值

**示例：**
```
✓ "Introducing AI Batch Processing"
✓ "How It Works: 5 Simple Steps"
✓ "Real-World Use Cases"
✓ "Getting Started Today"

✗ "Overview"
✗ "Details"
```

**数量：** 0-3 个

## 六、品牌语气规范

**News 类文章的独特语气：**

| 是 | 不是 |
|----|------|
| 兴奋的 | 夸张的 |
| 优势导向的 | 功能罗列的 |
| 可信的 | 过度承诺的 |
| 邀请性的 | 推销的 |
| 透明的 | 隐藏局限的 |

**示例对比：**

❌ **过度营销：**
"This absolutely revolutionary feature will change everything forever! You've
never seen anything like this before. Sign up NOW or miss out on the future of AI!"

✅ **品牌语气：**
"AI Batch Processing is now live—and it's a game-changer for teams working at
scale. Upload 100 photos at once, apply consistent AI styling, and save hours
on manual work. Ready to try it? Log in to alici.ai and find it in the Create menu."

## 七、特殊注意事项

### 与产品团队协作

**发布前必须确认：**
- 功能描述准确无误
- 定价和可用性最新
- 访问路径正确
- 限制和要求清晰

**理想流程：**
1. 草稿完成后，发送给产品团队审阅
2. 根据反馈调整技术细节
3. 发布前最终确认

---

### 时效性管理

**发布时机：**
- 与功能上线同步发布
- 或提前 1-2 天预告（"Coming Soon"）

**更新策略：**
- 如果功能有重大更新，发布新的 News 文章
- 在原文章顶部添加更新说明链接
