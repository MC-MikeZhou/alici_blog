A YouTube Shorts thumbnail is a small preview image that represents your video content. It is what viewers see prior to clicking on your Shorts, so it is important that you be able to capture attention right away. YouTube Shorts are simply short-form videos that are considered short-form as long as the video is under 60 seconds. Shorts videos are displayed in vertical format, whereas the thumbnail image for shorts videos is usually a still frame from the video or a custom image that you can upload to try to bring in viewers.

Although the thumbnail will not be as prominently displayed in the shorts feed as for regular YouTube videos, it still is displayed in places such as the YouTube homepage, search results, and suggested videos. A great thumbnail will optimize your chance of becoming noticed/clicked on, especially because YouTube automatically suggests Shorts for consumption based upon their interests.

Thumbnails are not just for mobile players. They also appear on the search results page and as suggested videos. All of those thumbnails help to grab attention and increase engagement.

*   **Increased visibility on YouTube search and suggested videos:**Thumbnails helps grab attention in YouTube search results, suggested videos, and homepages, encouraging users to click on your content.
*   **Improve click-through rate (CTR):**An attractive thumbnail can create a better click-through rate (CTR), convincing viewers to click on your video, which ultimately will lead to better engagement.
*   **Branding and recognition:**Thumbnails are the visual identity for your content and help identify your brand and distinguish your videos from other videos on the platform.
*   **Attracting audiences on mobile devices:**On mobile devices in particular, thumbnails are vital since they are often the first thing viewers see when scrolling through the feed. A good thumbnail can ultimately influence mobile engagement.
*   **Highlighting video content and message:**Thumbnails give a preview of what the video will be about, and allow creators to clearly express their video's main message and theme, which can help attract audiences that will engage with your content.

It's simple to add thumbnails to YouTube Shorts, whether you're uploading a new video or changing the thumbnail for an existing one.

STEP 1 1.   **Upload your video**

Open the YouTube app and tap the "+" icon located at the bottom center of your screen. Choose whether to select an existing video from your phone or record a new one. Once you've selected or recorded the video, tap "Done" to move on.

STEP 2 1.   **Add a****t****humbnail**

YouTube Shorts selects its thumbnail automatically in the video. To add a custom thumbnail, you need to add that thumbnail to the end of your video first. Tap "Add" and then choose the thumbnail you want. After you select it, tap the "tick" symbol, edit your video and tap "Next". On the "Add Details" page you tap the "pencil" icon on the image and select the frame you want to use for your thumbnail.

STEP 3 1.   **Upload the YouTube Short**

Finally, tap "Upload Short" to upload your video. You can find the uploaded video in the "You" section of your profile once it's processed.

The ideal YouTube Shorts thumbnail size is 1920 x 1080 pixels with a 9:16 aspect ratio. This size ensures your thumbnail looks sharp and professional across various devices, especially on mobile where Shorts are primarily viewed. The file size should not exceed 2MB, and the thumbnail should be in JPG, PNG, or GIF format for compatibility. A properly sized thumbnail enhances the visual appeal and ensures your content is displayed correctly on YouTube's platform, increasing the likelihood of it attracting clicks and boosting engagement. Always make sure your thumbnail is clear, vibrant, and easy to read.
