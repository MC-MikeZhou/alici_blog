## Quick Reference: The 7 Elements

When building any prompt, include:

| Element | Question to Answer | Example |
|---------|-------------------|---------|
| 1. Style | What visual style? | Photorealistic, anime, oil painting |
| 2. Subject | Who is the character? | Young woman, Keanu Reeves, elf warrior |
| 3. Clothing | What are they wearing? | Black trench coat, red kimono, armor |
| 4. Action | What pose/movement? | Standing with arms raised, combat stance |
| 5. Environment | Where are they? | Urban street, cherry blossom garden |
| 6. Lighting | What lighting mood? | Cinematic blue, soft natural, neon |
| 7. Camera | What shot type? | Full body, medium, close-up |

## Tips for Best Results

1. **Match your pose** - Generate a first frame that matches your video's starting pose exactly
2. **Be specific** - "long black trench coat" beats "coat"
3. **Consistent lighting** - Keep lighting similar between your recording and generated character
4. **Test in Standard** - Use 720p Standard mode for testing, switch to Pro for finals
5. **Use Partial mode** - When framings don't match perfectly between your video and generated image
