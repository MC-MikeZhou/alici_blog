| **Style** | Artistic style control | Ability to maintain specific aesthetics |
| **Character** | Face/body consistency across shots | Same person looks the same throughout |
| **Prompt** | How well it follows your prompts | Accuracy in interpreting text descriptions |

### v2.3 Design Decisions

1. **No Pricing in Capabilities Table**
   - Separates "what it can do" from "what it costs"
   - Pricing info retained in tool cards and Quick Comparison (now price-free too)
   - Aligns with user request: "only features and capabilities"

2. **Star Rating System**
   - More visual than numbers
   - ★★★★★ = Excellent (5/5)
   - ★★★★☆ = Very Good (4/5)
   - ★★★☆☆ = Good (3/5)
   - Easy at-a-glance comparison

3. **"Best For" Column**
   - Single-word/short phrase positioning
   - Helps readers quickly identify which tool fits their needs
   - Styled as colored tag for visual distinction

4. **Tooltip Hints**
   - Dimension headers have dotted underline (cursor: help)
   - Tooltip explains what each dimension measures
   - Improves accessibility for beginners
