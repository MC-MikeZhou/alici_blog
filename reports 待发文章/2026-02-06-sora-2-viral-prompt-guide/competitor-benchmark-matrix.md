## Pattern Library (what to copy)

1. InVideo pattern: "wide net + deep anchor"
- One broad list page captures variants, then deep sub-pages capture specific intents.
- Action for us: keep one canonical prompt guide, then spawn intent-specific satellites (templates, alternatives, troubleshooting).

2. Higgsfield pattern: "engineering framing for creators"
- Prompt guides include camera/motion/control primitives and operational language.
- Action for us: increase directive prompt grammar and explicit shot-control blocks.

3. OpusClip pattern: "proof-first growth storytelling"
- Case-study pages quantify outcomes and compress lessons into repeatable workflows.
- Action for us: add measurable experiment section and publish real benchmark deltas.
