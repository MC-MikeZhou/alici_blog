## Character 1: Balance Beam Gymnast

### Step 1: Generate Character (Nano Banana Pro)

```
A gymnast on a balance beam, professional leotard, arms by her side, neutral standing pose, athletic facility background, soft studio lighting, full body shot, 2K resolution
```

**Settings:**
- Model: Nano Banana Pro
- Resolution: 2K
- Outputs: 4 (pick your favorite)

### Step 2: Create Motion Reference (Kling 2.6)

```
hopping up and down on one leg with arms in the air
```

**Settings:**
- Model: Kling 2.6 (NOT Motion Control)
- Use neutral-pose image as start frame

### Step 3: Apply Motion (Kling 2.6 Motion Control)

- Mode: Match Image (for character consistency) or Match Video (for motion accuracy)
- Image: Your gymnast from Step 1
- Motion Video: Your generated video from Step 2
- Prompt: Leave blank (optional)

## Mode Selection Cheat Sheet

| Choose Match Image when... | Choose Match Video when... |
|---------------------------|---------------------------|
| Character details matter most | Motion accuracy matters most |
| Clip is under 10 seconds | Clip is 10-30 seconds |
| Unique textures/patterns | Complex choreography |
| Facial features are important | Spatial movement (walking forward/back) |

---

## Pro Tips

1. **Always start neutral** — Arms by sides, centered frame, no unusual poses
2. **Generate your own motions** — Don't hunt for reference videos; create them in Kling 2.6
3. **Skip the prompt** — Text prompts don't control motion, only background/lighting
4. **Test both modes** — Results vary; what works for one character may not work for another
