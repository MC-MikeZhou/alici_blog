## Step 2: Write Your Prompt (2 minutes)

The prompt is the most important part. It tells the AI exactly what video to create.

### Basic Prompt Structure

![The Perfect AI Video Prompt Formula: Subject + Action + Setting + Style](https://v3b.fal.media/files/b/0a8a752e/OblW90eXIqRKOrwYOiAIn_8465ae5a2fcc4be09c5983b7b11bbbb5.jpg)

```
[Subject] + [Action] + [Setting] + [Style]
```

### Example Prompts for Beginners

**Simple (Good for first attempt)**:
> A golden retriever running through a sunny meadow

**Medium (More specific)**:
> A golden retriever puppy running through a sunny meadow with wildflowers, slow motion, cinematic lighting

**Advanced (Professional results)**:
> A golden retriever puppy running joyfully through a sunlit meadow filled with purple wildflowers, slow motion footage, golden hour lighting, shallow depth of field, 4K quality

### What Makes a Good Prompt

| Element | Why It Matters | Example |
|---------|---------------|---------|
| **Subject** | Tells AI what to show | "a robot", "a woman", "a car" |
| **Action** | Creates movement | "walking", "dancing", "flying" |
| **Setting** | Provides context | "in a forest", "on Mars", "in a kitchen" |
| **Style** | Sets the mood | "cinematic", "cartoon", "vintage" |
| **Quality** | Improves output | "4K", "high detail", "professional" |

### Prompts to Avoid

- ❌ Too vague: "a cool video"
- ❌ Too complex: "a man walks into a bar, orders a drink, talks to three different people, then leaves and drives home"
- ❌ Copyrighted content: "Spider-Man fighting Darth Vader"

**Pro Tip**: Start simple. Your first prompt should be one sentence with a clear subject and action.

## Step 3: Generate Your Video (2 minutes)

Now the fun part—watching AI create your video.

### Generation Process

1. **Paste your prompt** into the text box
2. **Select settings** (if available):
   - Duration: Start with 5 seconds
   - Aspect ratio: 16:9 for landscape, 9:16 for vertical
   - Quality: Standard is fine for testing
3. **Click Generate**
4. **Wait 30-90 seconds** (generation time varies by platform and model)
5. **Preview your video**
6. **Download** if you're happy, or regenerate with a modified prompt

### What to Expect

Your first video might not be perfect. That's normal. AI video generation is iterative—you'll improve your results by:

- Adjusting your prompt based on the output
- Trying different style keywords
- Experimenting with different AI models

### Common First-Time Results

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Video looks weird | Simplify your prompt |
| Wrong style | Add style keywords like "cinematic" or "realistic" |
| Motion looks unnatural | Try a different model (Kling for realistic, Pika for stylized) |
| Too short | Upgrade to paid tier or use video extension features |

AI can generate realistic human motion, but results vary. For best results:
