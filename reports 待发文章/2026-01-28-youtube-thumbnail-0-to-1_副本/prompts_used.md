# Prompts Used


- case-question-hook: YouTube thumbnail concept: central short question text on clean, high-contrast background; optional person to the side with subtle expression; minimal words; strong color contrast; mobile readable.
- case-before-after: YouTube thumbnail concept: split screen before/after layout; clear visual transformation; minimal labels; high clarity; balanced composition; works on mobile.
- case-vs-comparison: YouTube thumbnail concept: left vs right comparison; two subjects contrasted; subtle 'VS' between; clean background; avoid clutter; strong separation colors.
- case-closeup-emotion: YouTube thumbnail concept: close-up facial reaction; expressive eyes; blurred simple background; no or minimal text; high contrast and clarity.
- case-product-feature: YouTube thumbnail concept: product close-up as hero; centered or rule-of-thirds; crisp lighting; high contrast; minimal supporting text.
- case-howto: YouTube thumbnail concept: how-to tutorial; show final outcome or main action; simple composition; few words highlighting the promise; mobile legibility.
