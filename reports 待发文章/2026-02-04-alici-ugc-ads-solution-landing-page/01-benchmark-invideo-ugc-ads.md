# InVideo “UGC Ads” Page — Reusable Persuasion & IA (Creator/Marketer View)

Source URL (for manual review): `https://invideo.io/make/ugc-ads/`

This doc is intentionally **pattern-based** (so we can reuse it for Alici) and should be validated by opening the page and checking the section order.

## 1) Who this page is for (implied by the offer)
- Performance marketers who need more winning ad creatives faster
- Ecommerce founders/operators who want UGC-style ads without filming
- Agencies/creative strategists producing high-volume variations
- UGC creators who want to productize output (scripts, storyboards, fast edits)

## 2) Job-to-be-done (JTBD)
“Help me ship UGC-style video ads that look native to TikTok/Reels/Shorts—fast enough to test angles, hooks, and offers at scale.”

## 3) The persuasion chain (what the page tries to make you believe)
1. UGC ads work (definition + benefits)
2. Making UGC ads is slow/costly (pain)
3. AI can compress the workflow (solution category)
4. This product is end-to-end (feature bundle narrative)
5. It fits your specific use case (use-case cards)
6. It’s easy to start (3-step HowTo)
7. Others trust it (social proof)
8. Your remaining doubts are answered (FAQ)
9. You should try now (CTA)

## 4) Reusable page modules (IA blocks)
### A) Hero (positioning + conversion)
- H1: “UGC ads” outcome statement
- Subhead: speed + simplicity + where it’s used (TikTok/Reels/Shorts, ecommerce)
- Primary CTA: start/try/generate
- Secondary CTA: examples/templates (for evaluators)

### B) “Solution = bundled capabilities” narrative
Pattern: list capabilities as components of one workflow.
- Inputs (product details, existing assets)
- Creative generation (scripts/storyboards/variations)
- Production (video generation/editing)
- Export & reuse (variants, aspect ratios, versions)

Why it works:
- It reduces buyer anxiety about missing steps.
- It signals the product is “platform” not “feature.”

### C) Use-case cards (intent router)
Use-case cards exist for:
- Relevance (“this is exactly what I need”)
- SEO expansion (each card can become a long-tail page)
- UX navigation (don’t make people scroll for their scenario)

### D) Why UGC (education block)
The “why” section upgrades the page from:
- tool page → solution page
- feature evaluation → category understanding

Important: this block must avoid unverified performance claims.

### E) How-to in 3 steps (friction reducer)
“Input → generate → export” is a universal cognitive model.
For AEO, it’s also perfect for `HowTo` schema.

### F) Social proof (trust layer)
Common formats:
- customer quotes
- “used by” logos
- rating badges
- usage counters

### G) FAQ (query coverage + objection handling)
FAQ should cover:
- definition (“what are UGC ads?”)
- why (“why do they work?”)
- how (“how do I make them?”)
- platform fit (“for TikTok/Reels?”)
- constraints (copyright, policies, disclosure)

## 5) What to copy vs what to avoid (for Alici)
### Copy
- Above-the-fold Key Takeaways (AEO + fast scanning)
- Use-case hub model (scales content + SEO)
- 3-step HowTo (schema + usability)
- Templates/examples (most persuasive “proof” we can ship without claims)

### Avoid
- Capability inflation (claiming actor marketplaces, licensed libraries, or voice cloning unless real)
- “X% uplift” claims without data
- Generic marketing fluff that reduces answer-engine extractability
