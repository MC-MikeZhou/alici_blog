## Visual Prompt Pack

### 疑问钩子（无人物极简）
A minimal 16:9 YouTube thumbnail with a solid, high-contrast background (teal vs navy). Center a 3–5 word Chinese question in bold Sans font, very large, generous margins, no extra icons. Clean, premium, modern.
[Style: minimalist, high-contrast | Ratio: 16:9]

### Before / After 分割
Split-screen 16:9 thumbnail with a thin center divider. Left=Before: dull colors, slightly messy; Right=After: vibrant, crisp, improved. White balance matched on both sides; key differences close to the divider for quick comparison.
[Style: clean, symmetric | Ratio: 16:9]

### VS 对比（中性但强烈）
Two symmetric subjects on left and right, a subtle “VS” in the middle, and 1–2 short traits under each subject (e.g., “更快”, “更稳”). Neutral background, strong contrast, no winner implied.
[Style: balanced, strong contrast | Ratio: 16:9]

### 行动定格（Action Shot）
Capture a decisive moment (e.g., object mid-air, tool in motion), sharp details, cinematic lighting. Reserve space for a short 3–5 word headline; avoid clutter and keep the main action clear.
[Style: cinematic, dynamic | Ratio: 16:9]
