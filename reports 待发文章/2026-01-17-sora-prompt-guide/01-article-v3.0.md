## Understanding Prompt Structure for Sora 2

The foundation of exceptional Sora 2 outputs lies in understanding how to structure your prompts. This section breaks down the two core strategies and introduces the 7-element framework that professionals use.

### Two Prompt Strategies: Descriptive vs. Directive

Sora 2 responds to two distinct prompting approaches:

**1. Descriptive Strategy**
Focuses on *what the AI should show* through rich sensory details. Best for atmospheric, mood-driven scenes.

**Example:**
```
A fog-shrouded forest at dawn, soft golden light filtering through ancient oak trees,
creating long shadows across moss-covered ground.
```

**2. Directive Strategy**
Focuses on *how the AI should film* by specifying camera movements, angles, and technical details. Best for dynamic, action-oriented sequences.

**Example:**
```
Drone shot ascending from ground level, revealing a coastal city at sunset.
Camera rises smoothly over beachfront buildings, then banks right to follow the
shoreline as waves crash below.
```

Most professional prompts blend both strategies, using descriptive elements to set the scene and directive elements to control the visual storytelling.

### The 7-Element Prompt Framework

Every high-quality Sora 2 prompt should address these seven elements:

#### 1. **Format & Duration**
Specify the video style and length expectations.

**Examples:**
- "Cinematic 4K footage, 45 seconds"
- "Documentary-style handheld, 30 seconds"
- "Time-lapse sequence, 20 seconds"

#### 2. **Camera Movement & Angles**
Define how the camera moves through the scene.

**Examples:**
- "Slow dolly-in from wide shot to medium close-up"
- "Steadicam following subject from behind"
- "Static low-angle shot looking up"
- "Orbital drone shot circling the subject"

#### 3. **Location & Environment**
Establish the physical setting with specific details.

**Examples:**
- "Abandoned Art Deco theater, velvet seats covered in dust"
- "Minimalist Tokyo apartment, floor-to-ceiling windows overlooking neon streets"
- "Rocky Pacific coastline, tide pools reflecting overcast sky"

#### 4. **Lighting & Time of Day**
Control mood and atmosphere through light quality.

**Examples:**
- "Golden hour backlight creating rim glow on subject"
- "Harsh midday sun creating stark shadows"
- "Soft overcast light, even exposure"
- "Neon signs casting colored light on wet pavement"

#### 5. **Motion & Action**
Describe what moves within the frame.

**Examples:**
- "Subject walks slowly toward camera, coat billowing in wind"
- "Leaves falling in slow motion"
- "Crowds moving in time-lapse blur while subject stands still"

#### 6. **Subject & Character Details**
Specify who or what appears in the scene.

**Examples:**
- "Woman in her 30s, wearing vintage leather jacket, confident expression"
- "Tabby cat sitting on window sill"
- "Classic 1960s Mustang, cherry red with chrome details"

#### 7. **Audio/Mood Indicators** *(Optional but powerful)*
While Sora 2 generates video only, audio descriptors inform the visual tone.

**Examples:**
- "Tense silence, as if waiting for something to happen"
- "Peaceful, meditative atmosphere"
- "Energetic, fast-paced mood"

> **📌 Citable**: The 7-element prompt framework—Format, Camera, Location, Lighting, Motion, Subject, and Audio/Mood—provides a systematic approach to crafting Sora 2 prompts that consistently produce cinematic results.

## Step-by-Step: Creating Your First Sora 2 Video

### Step 1: Define Your Video's Purpose

Start by clarifying your objective:

- **Product showcase**: Emphasize lighting and camera angles that highlight details
- **Storytelling**: Focus on character, emotion, and narrative progression
- **Atmospheric/B-roll**: Prioritize mood, environment, and visual aesthetics
- **Demonstration**: Use clear camera movements that show processes step-by-step

**Example:** For a product showcase of a luxury watch, your purpose is to highlight craftsmanship through close-up details and elegant lighting.

### Step 2: Choose Your Primary Strategy

Decide whether your video needs:

- **Descriptive approach** (mood-focused): Use for atmospheric scenes, establishing shots, ambient b-roll
- **Directive approach** (camera-focused): Use for dynamic action, specific camera movements, technical shots
- **Blended approach**: Combine both for complex scenes requiring both atmosphere and precise camera work

**Example:** For the luxury watch, use a blended approach—directive for the camera (macro close-up dolly) combined with descriptive for the setting (marble surface, soft studio lighting).

### Step 3: Build Your Prompt Using the 7-Element Framework

Work through each element systematically:

**Element-by-element construction:**

1. **Format**: "Cinematic 4K macro footage, 30 seconds"
2. **Camera**: "Slow forward dolly shot, starting from 45-degree angle, gradually moving to overhead view"
3. **Location**: "Luxury watch resting on polished black marble surface"
4. **Lighting**: "Soft studio key light from left creating gentle highlights on watch case, subtle fill light from right reducing shadows"
5. **Motion**: "Camera movement only, watch remains static, second hand ticking smoothly"
6. **Subject**: "Swiss automatic watch with skeleton dial revealing golden gears, sapphire crystal face, blue leather strap"
7. **Mood**: "Sophisticated, timeless elegance, emphasizing precision and craftsmanship"

**Combined prompt:**
```
Cinematic 4K macro footage, 30 seconds. Slow forward dolly shot, starting from
45-degree angle gradually moving to overhead view. Luxury Swiss automatic watch
with skeleton dial revealing golden gears, sapphire crystal face, blue leather
strap, resting on polished black marble surface. Soft studio key light from left
creates gentle highlights on watch case, subtle fill light from right reduces
shadows. Second hand ticks smoothly. Sophisticated atmosphere emphasizing
precision and timeless craftsmanship.
```

### Step 4: Generate and Refine

1. **Submit your prompt** to Sora 2
2. **Review the output** for these quality markers:
   - Camera movement matches your directive
   - Lighting creates the intended mood
   - Subject appears as described
   - Duration and pacing feel appropriate

3. **Iterate if needed** by adjusting specific elements:
   - Too dark? Add "increase key light intensity" or "brighter ambient lighting"
   - Camera moving too fast? Specify "slow, gradual dolly movement"
   - Missing details? Add more specific subject descriptors

### Step 5: Advanced Techniques

Once comfortable with basics, try these professional techniques:

**Technique 1: Camera Transition Prompts**
```
Opening with static wide shot of mountain lake at dawn, hold for 5 seconds,
then begin slow forward dolly toward water's edge, gradually lowering camera
angle until water fills lower third of frame, 40 seconds total.
```

**Technique 2: Time-Based Action**
```
Woman enters coffee shop, orders at counter (first 10 seconds), receives coffee
and walks to window seat (seconds 10-20), sits and looks outside pensively
(final 15 seconds), 45 seconds total, continuous steadicam following action.
```

**Technique 3: Atmospheric Layering**
```
Misty forest trail at sunrise. Thick fog rolls between trees, partially
obscuring background. Shafts of golden light pierce through canopy creating
god rays. Particles of dust and pollen float through light beams. Camera
slowly tracks forward down the path, revealing more trees emerging from fog.
Mysterious, ethereal mood.
```

## Common Prompt Mistakes to Avoid

### Mistake 1: Overloading with Visual Elements

**Bad Example:**
```
A busy Tokyo street with neon signs, people walking, cars driving, rain falling,
street vendors, bicycles, dogs, children playing, cherry blossoms falling, and
a helicopter overhead.
```

**Why it fails:** Too many competing elements create visual chaos. Sora 2 struggles to prioritize.

**Better Approach:**
```
Rainy Tokyo street at night, neon signs reflecting in wet pavement. Camera
tracks a lone pedestrian with umbrella walking through the empty street,
passing a closed street vendor stall.
```

### Mistake 2: Vague Atmospheric Descriptions

**Bad Example:**
```
A beautiful sunset scene with nice colors and a good mood.
```

**Why it fails:** "Beautiful," "nice," and "good" provide no actionable guidance. AI can't visualize subjective adjectives.

**Better Approach:**
```
Coastal sunset with deep orange sky transitioning to purple at zenith. Calm
ocean reflects golden light. Long exposure effect smooths water surface.
Peaceful, contemplative atmosphere.
```

### Mistake 3: Ignoring Camera Movement

**Bad Example:**
```
A mountain landscape with snow and trees.
```

**Why it fails:** No camera specification results in generic, often static shots.

**Better Approach:**
```
Slow aerial drone shot ascending above snow-covered mountain forest, starting
at treetop level and rising to reveal distant peaks, 35 seconds.
```

### Mistake 4: Contradictory Instructions

**Bad Example:**
```
Fast-paced action scene with slow, contemplative mood and rapid camera movements
that are also smooth and gradual.
```

**Why it fails:** "Fast-paced" contradicts "slow, contemplative," and "rapid" contradicts "smooth and gradual."

**Better Approach:**
```
Intense action scene with rapid but controlled camera movements—quick pans
between subjects with brief moments of slow-motion emphasis on key actions,
maintaining tension throughout.
```

### Mistake 5: Neglecting Lighting Details

**Bad Example:**
```
A person sitting in a room.
```

**Why it fails:** No lighting guidance means unpredictable results—could be bright, dark, warm, cool, or flat.

**Better Approach:**
```
Person sitting at desk in dimly lit study, single desk lamp creating warm pool
of light on workspace, face partially in shadow, creating film noir atmosphere.
```

## Optimizing Prompts for Different Video Types

### Product Videos

**Focus on:** Lighting, camera angles that highlight features, clean backgrounds

**Template:**
```
[Camera movement] of [product] on [surface/background]. [Lighting description]
highlights [key features]. [Duration], [visual style]. [Mood/brand alignment].
```

**Example:**
```
Slow rotating close-up of wireless headphones on matte white surface. Soft
overhead lighting with subtle rim light emphasizes metallic finish and smooth
curves. 25 seconds, minimalist product photography style. Modern, premium feel.
```

### Narrative/Storytelling Videos

**Focus on:** Character details, sequential action, emotional atmosphere

**Template:**
```
[Camera movement] following [character description] as they [action sequence].
[Environment]. [Lighting/time of day]. [Emotional tone]. [Duration].
```

**Example:**
```
Handheld steadicam following young woman in rain coat as she walks through
autumn park, kicks through fallen leaves, then stops to look up at overcast
sky. Golden afternoon light filtered through clouds. Melancholic but hopeful
mood. 40 seconds.
```

### B-Roll/Atmospheric Footage

**Focus on:** Environment, mood, subtle movements, visual aesthetics

**Template:**
```
[Camera type/movement] of [environment/scene]. [Atmospheric details].
[Lighting quality]. [Subtle motion]. [Mood descriptors]. [Duration].
```

**Example:**
```
Static wide shot of empty cafe interior before opening. Chairs still on tables,
morning light streaming through large windows creating long shadows across
hardwood floor. Dust particles visible in light beams. Quiet, anticipatory
atmosphere. 20 seconds.
```

### Action/Dynamic Scenes

**Focus on:** Camera movement, motion blur, energy, pacing

**Template:**
```
[Dynamic camera movement] capturing [action]. [Environment]. [Lighting for
drama/contrast]. [Motion characteristics]. [Energetic mood]. [Duration].
```

**Example:**
```
Tracking shot from driver's perspective racing through neon-lit cyberpunk city
at night. Camera mounted low, capturing rapid movement past glowing storefronts
and holographic advertisements. Motion blur on background, sharp focus on
foreground details. High-energy, futuristic feel. 35 seconds.
```

> **📌 Citable**: Different video types require distinct prompt optimization strategies—product videos emphasize lighting and angles, narrative videos focus on character and sequence, while b-roll prioritizes atmosphere and subtle environmental details.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue: Video Doesn't Match Expected Duration

**Cause:** AI interpreted pacing differently than intended

**Solution:** Add explicit pacing instructions
```
BEFORE: "Person walking through park, 30 seconds"
AFTER: "Person walking slowly through park, taking 30 seconds to move from
park entrance to central fountain, leisurely pace"
```

### Issue: Camera Movement Too Jerky or Unstable

**Cause:** Missing stabilization or smoothness descriptors

**Solution:** Specify stabilization method
```
BEFORE: "Moving shot of cyclist"
AFTER: "Smooth gimbal-stabilized tracking shot following cyclist, maintaining
consistent distance"
```

### Issue: Lighting Too Flat or Unpredictable

**Cause:** No lighting direction specified

**Solution:** Define light sources and quality
```
BEFORE: "Interior office scene"
AFTER: "Interior office scene lit by large window on left (soft natural
daylight) and warm desk lamp on right, creating dimensional lighting"
```

### Issue: Scene Too Generic or Lacks Personality

**Cause:** Insufficient specific details

**Solution:** Add unique environmental and character details
```
BEFORE: "Woman in cafe"
AFTER: "Woman in her 60s with silver hair, wearing emerald green cardigan,
sitting in vintage cafe with brass fixtures and worn leather booths, reading
handwritten letter"
```

### Issue: Too Much Happening at Once (Visual Overload)

**Cause:** Too many simultaneous actions

**Solution:** Simplify and focus on 1-2 primary elements
```
BEFORE: "Busy market with vendors selling, people shopping, cars passing,
music playing, flags waving, children running"
AFTER: "Market scene focused on elderly vendor arranging colorful spices in
wooden bowls, with blurred background activity suggesting busy market
atmosphere"
```
