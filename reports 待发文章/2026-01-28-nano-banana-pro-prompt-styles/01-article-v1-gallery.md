# Nano Banana Pro: 11 Photography Styles for AI Portraits

## One Prompt Template, 11 Iconic Looks

A single thread by [@mitch0z](https://x.com/mitch0z) pulled 2.1M views by showing what happens when you feed Nano Banana Pro the names of legendary photographers. The formula is dead simple -- and every prompt below is yours to copy.

## The Prompt Formula

Every style in this gallery follows a three-part template:

```
[person description], in the style of [photographer/brand name], [scene and lighting]
```

**Person description** sets your subject -- age, hair, outfit, pose. **Style reference** is the photographer or brand whose aesthetic you want. **Scene and lighting** locks in the environment, mood, and technical feel. Swap any part and the output shifts completely.

## 11 Styles Gallery

### 1. American Apparel

![American Apparel style](01-american-apparel-a.jpg)

**The Look**: Clean, minimal, 90s casual -- think bare studio walls, relaxed poses, and zero visual clutter.

**Try This Prompt**:

```
A young woman with dark hair in a casual outfit, in the style of American Apparel photography, minimal white background, natural lighting, relaxed pose
```

## FAQ

**Can I mix two photographer styles in one prompt?**
Yes. Try something like "in the style of Helmut Newton meets David LaChapelle" -- Nano Banana Pro handles blended references, though one style usually dominates.

**Do I need to describe my AI influencer every time?**
Keep a base description saved (age, hair color, key features) and paste it into the person slot of each prompt. This maintains consistency across styles.

**Are these the exact prompts from the original thread?**
