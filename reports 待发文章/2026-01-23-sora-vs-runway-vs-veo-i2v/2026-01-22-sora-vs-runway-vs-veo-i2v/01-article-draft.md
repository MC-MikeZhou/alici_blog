## Prompt Techniques: How to Write Effective Image-to-Video Prompts

Image-to-video prompting differs fundamentally from text-to-video. You're not describing what's in the image (the AI can see that)—you're directing motion, camera work, and timing.

### Sora 2: Segmented Format

Sora 2 uses a segmented prompt structure that separates visual context, motion directives, and audio cues:

**Structure**:
```
[VISUAL CONTEXT]
Describe the scene's mood and style (not what's visible).

[MOTION DIRECTIVES]
Specify camera movement, subject actions, physics interactions.

[AUDIO CUES] (optional)
Environmental sounds, dialogue, music mood.
```

**Example** (Portrait Animation):
```
VISUAL: Professional corporate headshot, warm studio lighting
MOTION: Subject makes subtle eye contact with camera, slight head tilt,
        natural breathing movement. Camera maintains fixed position.
AUDIO: Quiet office ambiance, soft fabric rustle
```

**Core Principle**: Sora's world state persistence means it remembers spatial relationships. Focus on physics-driven motion rather than aesthetic description.

---

### Runway Gen-4.5: Action-Focused Template

Runway performs best with concise, action-focused prompts that describe what happens, not what's already in the image.

**Structure**:
```
[Camera movement] shot of [subject action] in [environment context]
```

**Example** (Product Reveal):
```
Slow push-in on product rotating 45 degrees clockwise,
soft studio lighting reveal, clean white background
```

**Core Principle**: Runway's Motion Brush works best when text prompts stay simple—use brush for complex motion, text for camera/timing.

**Pro Tip**: Don't describe the image content. "Professional headshot of woman" is redundant—Runway sees the image. Instead: "Subtle smile emerges, eyes shift to camera."

---

### Veo 3.1: 5-Component Chain + Ingredients

Veo uses a structured 5-part format, enhanced by the Ingredients multi-image reference system.

**Structure**:
```
[Cinematography] + [Subject Focus] + [Action] + [Context] + [Style]
```

**Example** (Architectural Animation):
```
CINEMATOGRAPHY: Smooth dolly-in, shallow depth of field
SUBJECT: Modern glass building facade
ACTION: Morning sunlight sweeps across windows, reflections shift
CONTEXT: Urban downtown setting, golden hour
STYLE: Cinematic real estate commercial
```

**Ingredients Feature**: When using 3 reference images, order matters:
1. **Primary**: The main image to animate
2. **Style Reference**: Desired motion/lighting quality
3. **Composition Reference**: Framing/camera angle target

**Core Principle**: Veo's 3-image system maintains visual consistency better than text-only prompts for complex brand requirements.
