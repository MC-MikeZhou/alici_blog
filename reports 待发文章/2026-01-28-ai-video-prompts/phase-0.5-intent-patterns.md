## Extracted Intent Patterns (8 Patterns)

## Cross-Competitor Insights

### Common Themes Across All 4 Competitors:
1. **Year-Stamping**: 65% of content includes 2025/2026 in titles
2. **Tool Integration**: 80% reference specific AI models (Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway)
3. **Actionable Outcomes**: 70% promise specific results ("viral", "luxury-level", "Hollywood-quality")
4. **Speed/Efficiency**: 40% emphasize time-saving ("5 minutes", "instant", "single afternoon")

### Content Gap Analysis:
| Intent Pattern | Competitor Coverage | Opportunity Score |
|----------------|---------------------|-------------------|
| How-to + Specific Result | HIGH (InVideo, Higgsfield, Freepik) | Medium (saturated) |
| Number + Ultimate List | MEDIUM (InVideo, Higgsfield) | HIGH (differentiation possible) |
| Tool-Specific Guides | HIGH (all 4) | HIGH (tool-agnostic angle) |
| Comparison + Year | MEDIUM (Higgsfield only) | HIGH (underserved) |
| Monetization | MEDIUM (Higgsfield-dominated) | HIGH (alternative angles) |
| Ultimate Guides | MEDIUM (InVideo) | MEDIUM (requires authority) |
| Industry-Specific | LOW-MEDIUM (InVideo) | HIGH (vertical SEO) |
| Time-Saving | MEDIUM (InVideo) | MEDIUM (efficiency narrative) |

### Strategic Recommendations:
1. **Differentiation Angle**: Tool-agnostic "universal prompts" vs. tool-specific guides
2. **Authority Play**: Position as "prompt engineering fundamentals" vs. model news
3. **Practical Format**: Template-based vs. conceptual explanations
4. **SEO Opportunity**: "AI video prompts" space has limited comprehensive guides
