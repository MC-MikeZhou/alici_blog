## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is an AI UGC ad tool?

An AI UGC (user-generated content) ad tool uses artificial intelligence to create video ads that look like authentic creator content. Instead of hiring real influencers at $500–$5,000 per video, these tools generate talking-head videos with AI avatars or your own uploaded photos, complete with scripts and voiceovers. Most tools produce a finished ad in 2–5 minutes.

### Are AI UGC ads as effective as real creator UGC?

AI UGC ads are closing the performance gap rapidly. While real creator content still has an edge in perceived authenticity, AI-generated UGC delivers competitive click-through rates and significantly outperforms traditional brand ads. The main advantage is iteration speed—you can test 20–50 variations in hours, which often leads to better campaign performance through faster optimization cycles.

### How much do AI UGC tools cost per video?

Per-video costs range from roughly **$0.08** (Creatify at scale) to **$11** (Arcads AI). Most tools fall in the **$5–$10 per video range** on mid-tier plans. Free options exist—Creatify and InVideo AI both offer limited free tiers—but watermarks and resolution limits apply. Compare this to $500–$5,000 per video with real creators for clear ROI at scale.

### Can I use my own face instead of preset AI avatars?

Most tools require choosing from their library of preset AI avatars. **Alici.ai Video Super Agent** is the notable exception—you upload your own face photos and product images, and the AI creates UGC ads featuring your actual face. Some tools like Arcads and InVideo also offer limited "clone" features, but these typically require higher-tier plans and additional setup.

### Which AI UGC tool has the most realistic avatars?

**Arcads AI** currently leads in avatar realism, with AI actors trained on real human performances and fine-grained emotion controls for tone, expression, and delivery. **Creatify** has the largest avatar library (1,250+) but with less consistent quality across the range. For the most authentic approach, **Alici.ai** bypasses the avatar question entirely by using your real photos.
