### Step 2: Upload Your Photo

Not all photos animate well. Choose images with:

- **Clear subject**: A person, animal, or object that can logically move
- **Good resolution**: Minimum 1024x1024 pixels (ideally 2048x2048)
- **Natural composition**: Avoid heavily edited or collaged images
- **Implied motion**: Subjects that look like they could be mid-action work best

**Avoid**: Group photos with many people, abstract art, or images with text overlays.

For your first attempt, keep the prompt focused on one type of motion:

## Understanding Photo-to-Video AI

### How It Works

<!-- CITABLE_BLOCK: First Frame Principle -->
Photo-to-video AI operates on the "First Frame Principle": your uploaded image becomes the first frame of the video, and the AI model predicts subsequent frames based on learned patterns from millions of training videos. This differs fundamentally from text-to-video, where the AI must imagine the entire scene from scratch.
<!-- /CITABLE_BLOCK -->

Modern image-to-video models like Wan 2.1, Kling, and Runway use diffusion techniques combined with motion prediction. The AI:

1. **Analyzes your image** - Identifies subjects, depth, lighting, and scene context
2. **Predicts motion trajectories** - Determines how elements should move naturally
3. **Generates intermediate frames** - Creates smooth transitions between predicted positions
4. **Renders final output** - Produces a 4-10 second video clip at 24fps

## Advanced: Mastering Wan 2.1 Prompts

For professional-quality results, understanding prompt structure separates amateur animations from cinematic output. Wan 2.1 (and similar models) respond best to prompts that follow a specific framework.

### The 7-Element Prompt Framework

When your tutorial involves AI video generation, structure your prompts with these elements:

#### 1. Format and Style
Define the visual approach upfront:
- "Cinematic 4K footage"
- "Documentary style, natural lighting"
- "Film grain, 35mm aesthetic"

#### 2. Camera and Lens
Specify camera behavior:
- "Slow dolly forward"
- "Static shot, subtle handheld movement"
- "Smooth crane up reveal"

#### 3. Subject and Action
Describe what moves and how:
- "Woman in red dress turns toward window"
- "Coffee steam rises from ceramic mug"
- "Child's eyes widen with surprise"

#### 4. Motion Timing
Control the pace:
- "Movement occurs over 3 seconds"
- "Gradual acceleration"
- "Freeze at the end"

#### 5. Environmental Effects
Add atmosphere:
- "Dust particles float in light beam"
- "Curtains billow gently"
- "Shadows shift with passing clouds"

#### 6. Lighting Changes
Dynamic lighting sells realism:
- "Golden hour light intensifies"
- "Flickering candlelight"
- "Sun breaks through clouds"

#### 7. Audio Cues (for models that support it)
Guide the mood:
- "Ambient cafe sounds"
- "Wind rushing"
- "Soft piano in background"

### Complete Prompt Example

**Source image**: Portrait of a woman by a window at sunset

**Basic prompt** (beginner):
> "Woman turns head toward window"

**Advanced prompt** (professional):
> "Cinematic close-up, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field. Woman with auburn hair slowly turns her head toward the window over 2 seconds. Expression shifts from contemplation to gentle smile. Golden hour light intensifies on her face. Dust particles visible in the light beam. Subtle curtain movement from off-screen breeze. Film grain, warm color palette."

## Best Practices and Tips

### Photo Selection Criteria

The source image determines 60% of your output quality. Follow these standards:

**Resolution requirements**:
- Minimum: 1024 x 1024 pixels
- Recommended: 2048 x 2048 pixels
- Maximum benefit: 4096 x 4096 pixels (diminishing returns above this)

**Composition guidelines**:
- Subject should occupy 30-70% of frame
- Leave space in the direction of intended motion
- Avoid edge-to-edge subjects

**Technical quality**:
- Sharp focus on main subject
- Proper exposure (avoid blown highlights or crushed shadows)
- Minimal noise or compression artifacts

### Common Failure Causes and Fixes

| Problem | Cause | Solution |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Distorted faces | Low resolution source | Use 2K+ source images |
| Unnatural motion | Conflicting prompt instructions | Describe one motion type at a time |
| Flickering | Inconsistent lighting description | Add "consistent lighting" to prompt |
| Frozen background | No environmental motion specified | Add "subtle ambient motion" |
| Wrong direction | Ambiguous spatial terms | Use "camera left/right" not "left/right" |

### Optimal Settings by Use Case

**Social media content** (Instagram Reels, TikTok):
- Output: 720p, 4:5 or 9:16 aspect ratio
- Duration: 3-5 seconds
- Style: High contrast, punchy colors

**Professional portfolio**:
- Output: 1080p minimum, 16:9 aspect ratio
- Duration: 5-8 seconds
- Style: Cinematic, film grain optional

**Marketing materials**:
- Output: 1080p, multiple aspect ratios
- Duration: 3-6 seconds
- Style: Clean, brand-consistent
