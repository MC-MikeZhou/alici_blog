The Wan 2.6 Prompt Guide: 3 Rules to RememberNEW

Rule 1: Describe MOTION, Not Appearance

Wrong: "A beautiful woman with long brown hair..."
Right: "Woman turns her head slowly, soft smile appears, hair moves gently in breeze"

Rule 2: Use Real Camera Language

Wrong: "Make the camera move around"
Right: "Slow push-in toward subject, shallow depth of field"

Rule 3: One Action Per Prompt

Wrong: "Woman smiles, then laughs, then turns around, waves, and walks away"
Right: "Woman's expression softens into a gentle smile, slight head tilt"

FAQ (10 Questions)EXPANDED

1. How does photo-to-video AI actually work?

Your uploaded image becomes frame 1, and the model predicts subsequent frames based on your prompt and learned motion patterns.

2. Do I need editing or VFX skills?

No. The entire process happens inside the generator.

3. What's the most beginner-friendly camera movement?

Static shot is safest. Once comfortable, try slow push-in.

5. Can I create 15-second videos?

Yes. Wan 2.6 supports up to 15 seconds natively. Use multi-shot for longer sequences.

7. How do I make it more "cinematic"?

Add "shallow DOF", use push-in/pull-back movements, add "golden hour" lighting language.

9. Which mode: I2V vs T2V vs R2V?

For photo animation, always use I2V (Image-to-Video).
