Prompt Mastery: The 4-Part Formula ENHANCED

[Camera direction] + [Subject action] + [Scene detail] + [Mood/tone]
Component Example Purpose
Camera direction "Slow push-in camera" Tells AI how the viewpoint moves
Subject action "turns head gently and smiles" Defines what the main subject does
Scene detail "Soft afternoon light filters through" Adds environmental context
Mood/tone "Intimate, serene mood" Guides the overall feel

FAQ

Which preset should I use for my photo?

Match your photo type to the preset: Portrait Smile for headshots, Group Shot for 2-6 people, Product for e-commerce items, and Universal for landscapes or anything else.

How do I avoid distorted faces?

Use the Portrait Smile preset and add "subtle" or "gentle" to prompts. Avoid dramatic head rotations. Use 2048px+ resolution images.
