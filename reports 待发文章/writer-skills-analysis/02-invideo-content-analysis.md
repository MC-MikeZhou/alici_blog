## 7. Competitive Insights Summary

### invideo.io Strengths

1. **Content Depth**: 10,000+ word comprehensive guides establish topical authority
2. **Source Diversity**: 30+ citations in data articles = strong E-E-A-T
3. **Platform Coverage**: Dedicated content for YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, etc.
4. **Product Integration**: Natural tutorial integration (though biased)
5. **Schema Implementation**: BlogPosting + FAQPage confirmed

### invideo.io Weaknesses

1. **No AEO Optimization**: Missing Citable Blocks, Answer-First structure
2. **Promotional Bias**: "InVideo is the best" reduces objectivity trust
3. **No Evaluation Framework**: Inconsistent methodology for listicles
4. **Manual Process**: No visible automation or version control
5. **Homepage Metadata**: Missing dates/authors reduces freshness signals
