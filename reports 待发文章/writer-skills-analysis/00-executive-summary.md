## Bottom Line

**AliciBlog's 3 Writer Skills offer superior ROI** through automation, quality consistency, and AEO optimization. The 70%+ automation rate creates a defensible moat against manual content operations like invideo.io.

**invideo.io excels at** ultra-long content depth and extensive sourcing, but at significant human resource cost. Their product-centric approach works for SaaS conversion but sacrifices objectivity signals.

**Recommendation**: Continue investing in AliciBlog's automated pipeline. The marginal improvements from mimicking invideo.io's length/citation depth do not justify the ROI trade-off.
