## 4. E-E-A-T Investment Protection

### 4.1 Version Inheritance ROI

**Problem**: E-E-A-T content (case studies, testing data, author info) represents significant editorial investment.

**Without Version Inheritance**:
- v1 article: 5,500 words with 2 case studies, 4 sources, author info
- v2 rewrite: Loses case studies, sources, author (back to 4,800 words)
- Lost investment: ~3.75 hours of editorial work

**With Version Inheritance (v2.2)**:
- v1 article: Same content preserved
- v2 rewrite: Structure updated, E-E-A-T preserved
- Protected investment: 100% retention

| Scenario | E-E-A-T Content | Preserved | ROI Impact |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|------------|
| Without inheritance | 2 case studies, 4 sources | 0% | Rework needed |
| With inheritance | 2 case studies, 4 sources | 100% | Zero rework |

### 4.2 Material Gate ROI (case-roundup-writer)

**Problem**: Fabricated examples reduce E-E-A-T trust and require correction.

| Scenario | Quality | Review Needed | Rework Rate |
|----------|---------|---------------|-------------|
| **Without Material Gate** | Variable | High | 30-50% |
| **With Material Gate** | Consistent | Low | <5% |

**ROI Impact**: 6-10x reduction in quality assurance overhead.

## 5. Competitive Validation ROI

### 5.1 competitive-validator Skill Value

**Process**:
1. AEO score reaches target (e.g., >= 75)
2. competitive-validator searches Top 5 competitors
3. Quick AEO assessment of competitors
4. PASS if AliciBlog article >= 30% above competitor average

**ROI Benefit**:

| Metric | Without Validation | With Validation |
|--------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Publish confidence | Unknown | Quantified |
| Competitive position | Assumed | Verified |
| Quality baseline | Internal only | Market-relative |

### 5.2 Quality Assurance Funnel

```
100 articles written
    ↓ AEO Analyzer (75+ threshold)
85 articles pass first-time
    ↓ Auto-Improver (3 rounds max)
97 articles reach 75+
    ↓ Competitive Validator
92 articles PASS competitive check
    ↓ Publish
```

**Quality Yield**: 92% of articles meet both internal and competitive thresholds.
