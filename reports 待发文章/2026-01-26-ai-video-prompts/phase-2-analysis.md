## Recommendations for Phase 2.5 (Direction Clustering)

Given the **low viable keyword count** (4 instead of 50-60), the original Seed Mode plan needs adjustment:

### Option A: Pivot to Validated Keywords ⭐ (Recommended)
**Strategy**: Focus on the 4 viable keywords, cluster into 2 directions

**Direction 1**: "AI Video Prompts Guide" (General)
- **Primary**: AI video prompts (590 vol)
- **Secondary**: AI video prompt examples (110 vol)
- **Angle**: Comprehensive guide + actionable templates
- **Differentiation**: Cross-tool coverage (Sora, Kling, Runway, Veo)
- **Title**: "AI Video Prompts: Complete Guide + 20 Copy-Paste Templates [2026]"
- **Evidence Chain**:
  - 590 + 110 = 700 combined monthly searches
  - Top competitors are Reddit/Medium (beatable)
  - AI Overview for "examples" = high AEO value
  - PAA coverage = structured answer opportunity

**Direction 2**: "Sora AI Prompts" (Tool-Specific)
- **Primary**: Sora AI prompts (390 vol)
- **Secondary**: image to video AI prompts (110 vol, Sora supports this)
- **Angle**: Sora 2-specific prompt engineering + workflow
- **Differentiation**: Official guide exists, but we add practical examples + image-to-video
- **Title**: "Sora 2 Prompts: Complete Guide + Image-to-Video Workflow [2026]"
- **Evidence Chain**:
  - 390 vol with **highest CPC** ($4.24) = high commercial intent
  - OpenAI official guide (#4) = validates demand but leaves room for practical angle
  - Image-to-video (110 vol) = underserved workflow

**Output**: 2 locked directions ready for Phase 3

---

### Option B: Expand Validation ⚠️ (Higher Cost)
**Strategy**: Validate all 75 keywords from Phase 1 (not just priority 25)

**Pros**:
- May discover hidden gems in the remaining 50 keywords
- More comprehensive data for clustering

**Cons**:
- **Cost**: 75 keywords × $0.015 = **$1.125** (vs. $0.375 already spent)
- **Time**: ~3-5 minutes API calls
- **Risk**: Likely similar low-volume pattern (most platform-specific keywords have <100 vol)

**Recommendation**: Only pursue if user wants **exhaustive validation**. Otherwise, Option A is more efficient.

---

### Option C: Abort Seed Mode, Use Manual Route ⚠️
**Strategy**: Abandon seed keyword, use competitor URLs instead

**Rationale**:
- Original seed "7 Prompts" is a **content format**, not a **search demand pattern**
- DataForSEO shows real search is **tool-specific** (Sora, Veo, Kling) or **action-specific** (examples, guide)
- Manual Route can analyze **actual ranking articles** (e.g., OpenAI Sora guide, ltx.studio guide)

**Pros**:
- Align with proven demand (competitor URLs rank = demand exists)
- Skip costly keyword expansion

**Cons**:
- Loses Seed Mode's "discover new angles" benefit
- Becomes a "洗稿" (rewrite) workflow, not original research
