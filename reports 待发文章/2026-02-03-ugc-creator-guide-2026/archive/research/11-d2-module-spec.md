# D2 Module Spec (v2.0) — AI UGC Ads / Creative Testing (2026)

Module heading (locked): **AI UGC Ads in 2026: What They Are, What They Aren’t, and a Creator Workflow That Scales**

## Purpose (why this module exists)
Help UGC creator beginners:
- Understand **UGC Ads vs AI UGC Ads** without confusion
- Sell “**iteration**” (hooks + variants) instead of one-off videos
- Use AI as an accelerator for **storyboards + variants + draft scenes** while staying compliant

## Definition boundary (must include)
- **UGC Ads**: UGC-style creative used as paid ad assets (often for performance + creative testing).
- **AI UGC Ads**: UGC-style ad assets where some parts (script, visuals, voice, scenes) are **AI-assisted or AI-generated**.
- **Not a separate strategy**: AI UGC is a *production method*, not a marketing strategy like influencer marketing.
