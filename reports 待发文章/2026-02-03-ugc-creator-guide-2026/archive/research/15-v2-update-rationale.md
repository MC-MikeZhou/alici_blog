## What changed in v2.0 (vs v1.0 + vs typical competitor guides)

### 1) Stronger “money hook” (proof-first, not hype)
**Competitor gap:** many “UGC works” intros rely on generic claims without credible budget/demand data.  
**v2.0 fix:** first ~150 words include at least **2 citable data points**:
- creator economy growth signal (Goldman Sachs)
- 2026 marketing budget + video investment data (HubSpot)
- optional pricing benchmark (Billo) + trust lever (Nielsen)

### 2) Definitions are tightened (UGC / UGC Creator / UGC Ads / AI UGC Ads)
**Competitor gap:** definitions exist but are often brand-led and blur deliverables-only UGC with influencer distribution.  
**v2.0 fix:** “is / isn’t” boundaries + clear framing:
- UGC creator = deliverables for brands (usually not posted)
- influencer marketing = content + distribution/reach
- AI UGC Ads = production method inside UGC Ads (not a separate strategy)

### 3) The “UGC vs Influencer Marketing” section is corrected
**Competitor gap:** comparisons sometimes mistakenly treat AI UGC as a third strategy.  
**v2.0 fix:** table compares only **UGC vs influencer marketing** and puts AI UGC where it belongs: a method/tool.

### 4) The tutorial becomes deliverables-first (creator operating system)
**Competitor gap:** “how to become a UGC creator” posts often under-specify what to ship and how to ship it.  
**v2.0 fix:** a runnable workflow + templates:
- brief → script → shot list → edit recipe → delivery checklist
- usage rights checklist + a beginner-safe pricing line (not legal advice)

### 5) Niche selection is reframed as “deliverable shapes”
**Competitor gap:** niche advice is usually category-only (“beauty”, “fitness”) which isn’t actionable for beginners.  
**v2.0 fix:** niche entry points by **deliverable type** (demo, app walkthrough, before/after, comparison), each with script frameworks.

### 6) D2 module is purposeful: AI UGC Ads / creative testing loop
**Competitor gap:** AI sections drift into tool lists, or skip compliance and boundary definitions.  
**v2.0 fix:** one module that teaches:
- when to use AI-assisted UGC ads
- what to never do (misleading proof, fake testimonials, non-consensual likeness)
- a scalable iteration bundle (hooks × variants) beginners can sell

### 7) Compliance is integrated (not an afterthought)
**Competitor gap:** policy links appear only in footnotes (or not at all).  
**v2.0 fix:** explicit compliance section + links:
- FTC endorsement guidance
- TikTok branded content support + policy

### 8) Product integration is “workflow-native”, not spammy
**Competitor gap:** product mentions often read like inserts.  
**v2.0 fix:** alici.ai is placed where beginners actually struggle:
- generating scripts + shot lists fast (Video Super Agent)
- generating multi-scene drafts and variants for iteration
- optional deep links to internal AI video workflow posts
