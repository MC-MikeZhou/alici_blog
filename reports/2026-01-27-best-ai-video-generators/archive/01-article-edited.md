## How We Tested

Our evaluation went beyond surface-level comparisons. Here's exactly what we did:

### Test Prompt Categories

| Category | Example Prompt | What It Tests |
|----------|---------------|---------------|
| **Simple Product** | "A coffee cup on a wooden table, steam rising, morning light" | Basic rendering, lighting, physics |
| **Human Portrait** | "A woman in her 30s smiles naturally, eye contact with camera" | Facial realism, expression, skin texture |
| **Complex Action** | "A dancer performs a spinning leap, slow motion capture" | Motion fidelity, physics, temporal consistency |
| **Cinematic Scene** | "A car drives through a neon-lit city at night, rain on windshield" | Atmosphere, reflections, complex lighting |
| **Multi-Character** | "Two friends laugh together on a park bench, conversation" | Character consistency, interaction, dialogue |

### Scoring Process

1. **Blind review**: Outputs shuffled and scored without knowing which tool generated them
2. **Multiple reviewers**: 3 team members scored independently, averaged for final score
3. **Consistency check**: Same prompt run 3 times per tool to assess output reliability
4. **Real-world validation**: Select outputs shown to non-expert viewers for "would you notice this is AI?" feedback
