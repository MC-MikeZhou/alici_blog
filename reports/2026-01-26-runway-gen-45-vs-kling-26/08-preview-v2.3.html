Category Winners

Based on Curious Refuge's testing, here's how each tool performed across six controlled scenarios:

Best for Action and Water: Kling 2.6 WINNER

Why it wins: According to the testing, Kling 2.6 handles water splashes and character movement with impressive temporal consistency. In a test featuring a man sprinting through a stream with handheld camera tracking, Kling maintained natural water physics and smooth motion throughout the clip.

In contrast, Runway Gen 4.5 showed issues with the water wake looking "like he's an actual boat running on the water" and noticeable "diffusion noise" stuttering in background details.

Choose Kling 2.6 if: You're creating action sequences with water, rain, or fluid elements.
Avoid Kling 2.6 if: You need extremely long video clips (>10 seconds in a single generation).

Best for VFX (Particles and Smoke): Kling 2.6 WINNER

Why it wins: In the particle and smoke test featuring a spirit character with purple particles, both tools produced acceptable results. However, Curious Refuge noted Kling's smoke "billows feels a bit more realistic" while giving Runway "more like a six out of 10."

Best for Fire Effects: Kling 2.6 WINNER

Why it wins: Testing a woman running toward a burning barn, Kling 2.6 delivered what Curious Refuge described as "much much more cinematic and realistic" results.

Best for Dialogue and Lip-Sync: Google Veo 3.1 WINNER

Why it wins: For a two-person conversation test, Veo 3.1 "looks really good," followed the slow push-in prompt, and offers native lip-sync capability. Runway Gen 4.5 "does not give you sound."

Best for 2D Animation: Kling 2.6 WINNER

Why it wins: Testing a 2D character at a bus stop, Kling showed "temporal consistency definitely looks better." Runway's character "completely changes" during motion.

Best for 3D Animation: Kling 2.6 WINNER

Why it wins: In a 3D octopus character test, Kling "looks much better" and is "much more cinematic." Runway had "a lot of problems with the texturing."
