Manage your channel branding
============================

Brand your YouTube channel's identity by updating your profile picture, channel banner, and video watermark.

[Computer](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)[Android](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)[iPhone & iPad](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

More
1.   More

[Change your profile picture](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

Change your profile picture in YouTube Studio. Your profile picture is the image shown to viewers on your channel, videos, and publicly attributable actions across YouTube.

1.   Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).
2.   From the left menu, select **Customization**![Image 1](./assets/BET-S7led4e9J6SQ9_2JOUc8hXiNy26yzc23ZCTpMjJO4iA_z-NvRMZy2E5sJNfmL8g=w48-h48.png)![Image 2: and then](./assets/f0266215-9232-49d1-bb42-a3b46898e6f5.png)**Profile**.
3.   Click **CHANGE** and select an illustration or an image from your computer. Change the preset colors and crop of the illustration or the size of your uploaded image, then click **DONE**.
4.   Click **PUBLISH**. It may take a few minutes for your profile picture to update across YouTube.

### Crop your photo with assistive technology or keyboard

#### Crop your photo from a corner

1.   Navigate to select the corner of your photo.
2.   Use the arrow keys to crop the photo.

#### Move the entire crop square

1.   Navigate to select the entire crop square.
2.   Use the arrow keys to change the position of the crop square.

### Profile picture guidelines

Your profile picture must follow our [Community Guidelines](https://www.youtube.com/t/community_guidelines) and meet the following criteria:

*   JPG, GIF, BMP, or PNG file (no animated GIFs)
*   Image size should not exceed 15 MB.
*   Image that renders at 98 X 98 px.

[Change your banner image](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

### YouTube Creators

Your banner image shows as a background at the top of your YouTube page.

1.   Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/) .
2.   From the left menu, select **Customization**![Image 3](./assets/BET-S7led4e9J6SQ9_2JOUc8hXiNy26yzc23ZCTpMjJO4iA_z-NvRMZy2E5sJNfmL8g=w48-h48.png)![Image 4: and then](./assets/f0266215-9232-49d1-bb42-a3b46898e6f5.png)**Profile**.
3.   Click **CHANGE** and select an image. To make changes, select a preview and change the crop, then click **DONE**.
4.   Click **PUBLISH**.

**Note:** The same banner image is used across computer, mobile, and TV displays, but it shows differently depending on your device.

### [Official Artist Channels](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

Your banner image is only used on the computer and mobile devices. To update that banner, follow the YouTube Creator instructions above.

To update the banner you have on TV and on YouTube Music:

1.   Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).
2.   From the left menu, select **Profile**.
3.   Click ![Image 5](./assets/m-unw3sH189jYAZdjS2Kdmm3ZmaXOh0Wa6WGv2H-k8XpGJcWhcF7C3sZCJl2cn-zlJ_Q=h48.png) next to the profile photo and select an image. To make changes to an image, 
    *   Select **Preview** and edit the image.
    *   Click **DONE**.

4.   Click **SAVE** at the top right of the page.

### Banner image guidelines

Follow these recommendations for your banner to render properly:

*   Minimum dimension for upload: 2048 x 1152 px with an aspect ratio of 16:9.
*   At the minimum dimension, the safe area for text and logos: 1235 x 338 px.
*   Recommended dimension (especially for TV): 2560 x 1440 px.
*   Images should accommodate the entire screen for larger devices but will get cropped on certain views and devices.
*   Do not include any additional file embellishments (e.g. shadows, borders, and frames).
*   File size: 6 MB or smaller.

### **How to resize images**

You can use your computer's image editor or an online image resizer to resize the images. For example, you can use Preview on an Apple computer or [Microsoft Photos](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/help/27916) on Windows.

[Add your video watermark](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10456525?hl=en)

You can add a video watermark to your video to promote your channel’s branding.

1.   Sign in to [YouTube Studio](https://studio.youtube.com/).
2.   From the left menu, select **Customization**![Image 6](./assets/BET-S7led4e9J6SQ9_2JOUc8hXiNy26yzc23ZCTpMjJO4iA_z-NvRMZy2E5sJNfmL8g=w48-h48.png)![Image 7: and then](./assets/f0266215-9232-49d1-bb42-a3b46898e6f5.png)**Profile**.
3.   Select your display time: 
    *   **End of video:** The video watermark will show for the last 15 seconds of the video.
    *   **Custom start time:** The video watermark will begin showing at a time you choose.
    *   **Entire video:** The video watermark will show throughout the entire video.

4.   Click **CHANGE** and select an image. Change the size of your image, then click **DONE**.
5.   Click **PUBLISH**.

**Note:** Video watermarks aren’t available on videos set as made for kids. If you previously added a video watermark, but your video is now set as made for kids, viewers won’t see the watermark.

### Video watermark guidelines

Your video watermark must meet the following criteria:

*   Minimum 150x150 pixels.
*   Square image less than 1 MB in size.

### Availability

The channel watermark is available in landscape view on computers and mobile devices (not clickable on mobile). Channel watermarks do not show on custom [YouTube chromeless players](https://code.google.com/apis/youtube/chromeless_player_reference.html) or Adobe Flash.

### Video watermark metrics

You can get metrics in the [Subscription source](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9717879) report in YouTube Analytic.
