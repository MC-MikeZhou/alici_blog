## Pattern 1: Problem-Solution

**中文名**: 问题-解决方案

**结构**:
```
痛点陈述 → 传统解决方案的局限 → AI/新方案引入 → 文章承诺
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Target audience] traditionally face [pain point].
[Old solution] costs [money/time] and requires [skill/resource].

But [new technology/approach] is changing everything.
[Specific benefit] + [proof point].

In this guide, we'll [article promise].
```

**示例**:
> "Creating a professional-looking video traditionally requires expensive equipment, technical expertise, and significant time investment. Most businesses spend $1,000-$10,000 per video with agencies. But AI video generators are changing this..."

**适用场景**: 工具推荐文章, How-to 教程, 任何"解决问题"定位的内容

## Pattern 2: Data Hook

**中文名**: 数据钩子

**结构**:
```
震撼数据 → 趋势解读 → 机会/威胁 → 行动召唤
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Shocking statistic] ([Source, Year]).
[Market size/growth data].

[Implication for reader].
[Consequence of inaction OR opportunity for action].

[Article promise with specific number].
```

**示例**:
> "91% of businesses now use video as a marketing tool (Wyzowl, 2024). The global AI video generator market is projected to reach $1.5 billion by 2028. If you're not leveraging video, you're leaving money on the table. Here are 135 statistics that explain why..."

**适用场景**: Statistics 数据类文章, 趋势分析, 说服性内容

## Pattern 3: Pain Point Question

**中文名**: 痛点提问

**结构**:
```
共鸣问句 → 读者验证 → 问题放大 → 解决方案预告
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Relatable question about reader's struggle]?
[Validation data: "X% of [audience] face this" or "You're not alone"].

[Problem amplification: consequences, scale, emotional impact].

[Solution preview: "That's where [solution category] comes in..."]
```

**示例**:
> "Ever felt like coming up with an idea to post online is super hard? You're not alone—digital creators spend 1-5 hours weekly just on content ideation. The pressure to stay consistent across multiple platforms while maintaining quality is overwhelming most marketers. That's where AI tools come in..."

**适用场景**: Listicle 工具推荐, 面向个人用户的内容, 情感驱动型文章

## Pattern 4: Reframe

**中文名**: 重新定义问题

**结构**:
```
常规认知 → 现实揭露 → 问题重定义 → 新视角承诺
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Commonly held belief/expectation].

[Reality check: what actually happens].

The real question isn't '[old question].'
It's '[reframed question].'

[Promise to answer the new question].
```

**示例**:
> "Demos make every AI model look magical. Production reveals the truth. You've probably seen stunning AI video demos—Sora's cinematic clips, Runway's smooth transitions, Kling's realistic motion. But here's what those demos don't show: actual production challenges. The real question isn't 'which AI looks best in demos?' It's 'which one delivers in real workflows?' Let's find out..."

**适用场景**: Showdown 对决类, 打破迷思的内容, 高级/专家级读者

## Pattern 11: Methodology Authority

**结构**:
```
测试声明 → 评估维度 → 筛选结果 → 文章价值
```

**模板**:
```markdown
We tested [N+] tools to find the [final N] best.

Our evaluation criteria:
- [Criterion 1]
- [Criterion 2]
- [Criterion 3]
...

Here's what made the cut.
```

**示例**:
> "We tested 50+ AI video generators to find the 14 best. Our evaluation criteria: output quality, prompting efficiency, speed, usability, export options, pricing, reliability, privacy. Here's what made the cut..."

**适用场景**: 需要建立测试权威的榜单, AI 工具对比文章, "Tried & Tested" 定位的内容

## Pattern 16: Ideal Solution Criteria

**中文名**: 理想方案标准

**结构**:
```
竞品优点 → 竞品局限 → 理想标准定义 → 评测承诺
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Competitor] is great for [strength 1] and [strength 2].

But it might fall short if you need [pain point 1],
[pain point 2], or [pain point 3].

The perfect alternative should [criteria list].

We've evaluated [N] options. Here's what we found.
```

**示例**:
> "Videoscribe is great for whiteboard animations with its large stock library and offline functionality. But it might fall short if you need MP4 imports, longer videos, or advanced customization. The perfect alternative should allow users to [criteria]. We've evaluated 10 options. Here's what we found..."

**适用场景**: 竞品替代文章, Alternatives 榜单

## Pattern 19: Cost Barrier Hook

**中文名**: 成本障碍钩子

**结构**:
```
高成本问题标题 → 传统方法局限 → DIY 陷阱 → 规模问题 → 解决方案引入
```

**模板**:
```markdown
**The $[X]K Problem Most [Audience] Can't Solve**

For most [audience], [output] is not the problem—
the process around creating it is.

1. **The Cost Barrier**: Traditional [method] costs $[X]-$[Y]
2. **The DIY Trap**: [Learning curve pain point]
3. **The Scaling Problem**: [Scale multiplier challenge]

That's where [solution] comes in.
```

**示例**:
> "**The $10K Problem Most Brands Can't Solve** For most e-commerce brands, product videos are not the problem—the process around creating them is. 1. The Cost Barrier: Traditional production runs $5K-$15K per video 2. The DIY Trap: Learning Premiere takes months 3. The Scaling Problem: You have 200 SKUs. That's where Money Shot comes in..."

**适用场景**: 高价值 B2B 解决方案, 电商/产品视频内容

## Pattern 20: False Binary Challenge

**中文名**: 假二元论挑战

**结构**:
```
对立陈述 → 挑战非此即彼 → 重新定义标准 → 新视角承诺
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Surface-level observation about two approaches].

But here's what most people miss:
[Challenge the binary framing].

The real difference isn't [obvious factor].

```

The real difference isn't tools. It's intention, taste, and emotional clarity. Let's explore what actually makes ads resonate..."

**适用场景**: 思想领导力内容, 哲学/创意类文章

## Pattern 21: Production Pain Scenario

**中文名**: 制作痛点情景

**结构**:
```
情景描述 → 常见"意外需求" → 传统解决方案成本 → 新方案引入
```

**模板**:
```markdown
[Satisfying completion moment: "The [work] finally lands..."].
[The surprise request: "Then someone says..."].

Traditional solution: [logistics, costs, time].
The real issue: [it's not about quality, it's about variables].

That's what [solution] solves.
```

**示例**:
> "The performance finally lands. Everyone relaxes for a moment. Then someone says, 'What if we changed the person?' Traditional reshoots mean scheduling, logistics, and costs. Most of the time, it's not about fixing a bad idea—it's about shifting variables. That's what Performances solves..."

**适用场景**: 功能深度教程, 制作流程优化
